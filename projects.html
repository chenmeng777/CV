Research Projects

Project 1: Vibration and Noise Optimization of Gear Hybrid Transmission Systems

Overview:

  • Developed CFD, numerical tribology, and nonlinear dynamic models to characterize the lubrication, contact, and dynamic behaviors of a wet clutch-gear transmission system.
  • Established and experimentally validated a coupled gear-clutch-bearing dynamic framework for analyzing vibration and torque transmission characteristics of a twin-engine combining gearbox.

Key Results:

  • Published in Journal of tribology, 2026.
  • Analyzed the fluid behavior, temperature distribution, and pressure distribution within the wet clutch clearance during the disengaged state.
  • Characterized the effects of hydraulic pressure uncertainty on the tribo-dynamic performance of a wet clutch during engagement and quantified uncertainty propagation.
  • Developed an improved wet clutch torque transmission model considering high-speed friction and lubrication effects under dual-drive conditions, and evaluated its influence on twin-engine combining gearbox dynamics and vibration responses.

Project 2: CFD Analysis of Marine Propeller Flow Field

Overview:

  • Developed a three-dimensional CFD model of the P4119 marine propeller using ANSYS Fluent and predicted thrust, torque, and open-water characteristics.
  • Compared numerical predictions with published experimental data and analyzed pressure distributions, wake flow, and vortex structures.

Key Results:

  • Predicted thrust, torque, and open-water performance over a range of advance coefficients.
  • Benchmarked CFD predictions against published experimental measurements.
  • Visualized pressure contours, velocity fields, and wake vortex structures.
  • Demonstrated the applicability of CFD for marine propeller hydrodynamic performance evaluation.

Project 3: Lightweight Design and Optimization of Carbon Fiber Composite Structures for Large Telescopes

Overview:

  • Investigated the structural design and optimization of carbon fiber composite truss structures for large telescope applications through finite element modeling and optimization algorithms.
  • Developed optimized layup configurations to improve structural stiffness performance and established a finite element and experimental validation framwork for lightweight structural design.

Key Results:

  • Developed optimized composite layup configurations using sensitivity analysis and Evolutionary Algorithms.
  • Built FEM of composite truss members, tetrahedral elements, and the complete telescope primary mirror support structure.
  • Investigated structural stiffness, deformation behavior, natural frequencies, and vibration modes.
  • Validated the numerical models through mechanical loading and modal testing of fabricated composite specimens.
  • Verified the effectiveness of lightweight carbon fiber composite designs for large telescope structures.
