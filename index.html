Personal Introduction

Hello! I'm Meng Chen, a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Zhejiang University. My research focuses on tribology, nonlinear dynamics, and multi-physics modeling of mechanical transmission systems. I develop analytical, finite element, and CFD models to investigate wet clutch engagement, lubrication behavior, gear transmission dynamics, and thermal-fluid interactions. My work integrates uncertainty quantification and experimental validation to understand the coupled mechanisms of lubrication, contact, and structural dynamics in high-power transmission systems.
